BAB 01

My Family and Friends

Describing people dan possessive adjectives dalam kalimat sederhana.

MATERI 01

Describing People

Gunakan kata sifat seperti kind, friendly, smart, dan happy untuk menggambarkan orang.

Penjelasan & Contoh

My friend is kind. — Temanku baik. She is happy. — Dia senang.

MATERI 02

Possessive Adjectives

My, your, his, dan her menunjukkan kepemilikan.

Penjelasan & Contoh

This is my friend. This is her book. This is his bag.

BAB 02

Numbers and Time

Mengenal angka 21–50 dan menyebutkan waktu tepat.

MATERI 01

Numbers 21–50

Latih membaca angka dan mengenali pola puluhan.

Penjelasan & Contoh

21 = twenty-one, 30 = thirty, 45 = forty-five, 50 = fifty.

MATERI 02

Telling Time

Untuk jam tepat, gunakan pola “It is ... o'clock.”

Penjelasan & Contoh

It is 7 o'clock. — Sekarang pukul tujuh.

BAB 03

My Daily Routines

Mengenal kegiatan harian dan simple present untuk kalimat sederhana.

MATERI 01

Daily Activities

Kosakata kegiatan: wake up, take a bath, go to school, study, eat, dan sleep.

Penjelasan & Contoh

I wake up at 6 o'clock. I go to school at 7 o'clock.

MATERI 02

Simple Present Tense

Untuk kebiasaan sederhana, gunakan pola dasar seperti I + verb.

Penjelasan & Contoh

I study every day. I play after school.

BAB 04

Things in the House

Mengenal ruangan, perabot, dan posisi benda.

MATERI 01

Rooms and Furniture

Contoh ruangan: living room, bedroom, kitchen. Contoh benda: table, chair, bed.

Penjelasan & Contoh

The bed is in the bedroom. The table is in the kitchen.

MATERI 02

Prepositions of Place

In, on, under, dan next to menunjukkan posisi.

Penjelasan & Contoh
